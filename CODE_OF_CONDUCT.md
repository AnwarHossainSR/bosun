# Code of Conduct

bosun follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html). In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and experience level, and assume good intent.

Harassment, personal attacks and other unacceptable behavior can be reported to the maintainer, [@AnwarHossainSR](https://github.com/AnwarHossainSR), through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Every report is reviewed promptly, fairly and in confidence, and the maintainer may warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
